From "National Party Committees" to "State Committees": A Term's Path and the State's Tools

How the accused party behind "electronic committees" shifted in the Egyptian press, and what the state built in parallel

Overuse turned the term into an "empty signifier" thrown at any hashtag criticizing the government, and the ambiguity itself is a tool of digital authoritarianism

The term in the press (110 items, 2011–2021)

26 Feb 2011

First reference, in Youm7: pro-Mubarak National Democratic Party committees

2012–2013

During Morsi's year in power the charge moves to the Brotherhood, with Khairat El-Shater its "mastermind"

Since 2016

Pro-government papers openly celebrate state-backed committees, including a group led by Sherif Khairy

Later

Official discourse returns to blaming the Brotherhood, Turkey and Qatar for every opposition hashtag

The state's official tools

Jun 2014

Interior Ministry tender for a "social networks security risk monitoring" system

2014

Supreme Cybersecurity Council, with a committee to follow cyberspace and monitor "deviant" opinions

2019

Public Prosecutor's decree 2376 creates an office that opens social media accounts and acts as a "watchtower"

Source: Nermeen El-Sherif and Tarek Anwar, International Journal of Communication (2024); Legal Agenda (2014); Bassant Hassib and James Shires, Journal of Cybersecurity (2021); Arab Center for Research and Policy Studies (2020)
