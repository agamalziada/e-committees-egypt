One Week, One Narrative: From the Interior Ministry's Statement to the Prosecution's

How the phrase "media committee" travelled between 28 September and 7 October 2026

The phrase that began in the police statement before any of the six had been asked a single question became, a week later, an admission attributed to them in the prosecution's statement
ArrestsOfficial narrativeMedia and postsDefence
28 Sep

Arrest of Mohamed Ashraf Abu Omeira and Abdullah Qadri, then Islam Barakat, Omar Helal, Mohamed Mahmoud and Mohamed Adel

1 Oct

Interior Ministry statement: the page is "unlicensed and run from abroad" and part of the Brotherhood's "media committees"

1 Oct

Al-Masry Al-Youm reports the six were remanded for fifteen days; their lawyers deny it

2 Oct

Bawabat Al-Ahram builds an item on an old Essam El-Erian clip; Abdel Rahim Ali airs three recordings of undisclosed source on MBC Masr

3 Oct

Old posts attributed to an account in Abdullah Qadri's name circulate as "confessions"; the Interior Ministry vows action against "promoters of the allegations"

4 Oct

According to EIPR: journalists tell the State Security Prosecution they were blindfolded, beaten and electrocuted, and forced to read "confessions" on camera

7 Oct

Prosecution statement: one defendant "admitted" joining the "media committee"; forensic reports find them "free of injuries"
